# Civic Pulse – AI-Powered Civic Issue Reporting Platform

**Report. Track. Resolve.**

## About the Project

Civic Pulse is a web-based platform that aims to make reporting civic problems easier for the public and help government authorities manage complaints more effectively.

In our daily lives, we come across problems such as potholes, garbage accumulation, water leakage, drainage blockages, electricity failures, and damaged streetlights. Although people report these issues through different channels, getting updates and knowing whether the problem has been resolved can still be difficult.

We started working on Civic Pulse with the idea of bringing citizens and authorities together through a single platform where civic issues can be reported, monitored, and managed.

## What Does Civic Pulse Do?

The platform allows citizens to submit complaints by providing a description, photographs, and location details. The responsible authorities can review these complaints, assign them to the appropriate departments, and track the work until resolution.

We also plan to use AI to make complaint management smarter by identifying issue categories, detecting duplicate reports, and estimating the severity of problems.

## Main Features

### 1. Citizen Module
- Register and log in to the platform.
- Report civic problems with photos and descriptions.
- Add GPS location or enter an address manually.
- Get a unique ID for each complaint.
- Track the status of submitted complaints.
- View issues reported by other citizens.
- Support important complaints through community voting.
- Provide feedback after an issue is resolved.

### 2. Authority Dashboard
- View complaints submitted by citizens.
- Check complaint details, photographs, and locations.
- Verify complaints and assign them to the relevant departments.
- Assign work to field workers.
- Monitor complaint progress.
- Check completed work and uploaded evidence.

### 3. Field Worker Module
- View assigned complaints.
- Accept and update assigned tasks.
- Upload before-and-after photographs.
- Add remarks about completed work.

### 4. Admin and Higher Authority
- Manage users and departments.
- Monitor complaints across the platform.
- View pending and resolved issues.
- Track overdue complaints.
- Review escalated complaints.
- Monitor department performance.

### 5. AI-Based Features

We are planning to introduce AI to improve the complaint management process.

Some of the features we aim to implement are:

- **Issue Classification:** Identify the type of civic problem from uploaded images and descriptions.
- **Duplicate Detection:** Find complaints that may refer to the same issue.
- **Severity Prediction:** Estimate how serious a reported problem is.
- **Suspicious Report Detection:** Identify reports that may need additional verification.
- **Department Recommendation:** Suggest the correct department based on the complaint.

These AI features are part of our development plan and will be integrated and tested as the project progresses.

- Garbage accumulation
- Water leakage
- Drainage and sewage problems
- Electricity failures
- Streetlight issues
- Other public infrastructure problems

## How the System Works

1. A citizen reports a civic issue through the platform.
2. The complaint is recorded with its description, image, and location.
3. The system helps identify the issue category and priority.
4. The authority reviews the complaint.
5. The complaint is assigned to the responsible department.
6. A field worker receives the assigned task.
7. The field worker updates the work status and submits evidence.
8. The authority checks the completed work.
9. The citizen can provide feedback on the resolution.

If a complaint remains unresolved, it can be escalated to a higher authority.
